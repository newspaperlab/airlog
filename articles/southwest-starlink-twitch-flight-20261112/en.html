Southwest streams a game tournament from an Austin–San Diego flight

The TwitchCon demonstration used a Starlink-equipped special flight. Fleetwide installation is targeted for fall 2027.

Newspaper Lab · Published 2026-11-14
Source announced 2026-11-12

Southwest said on November 12 local time that Twitch creators competed and livestreamed from a Starlink-equipped special flight between Austin and San Diego. The airline cited an altitude of 35,000 feet, approximately 10.7 km. Its account confirms the event ahead of TwitchCon, with no exact takeoff time or connection measurements for the full stream.

Southwest operates scheduled passenger services primarily in the US. The event used an aircraft already equipped for the demonstration. The carrier targets fleetwide Starlink installation in fall 2027. For a traveler buying a flight now, the assigned aircraft and available service matter more directly than that fleet target. Identical aircraft types can include both retrofitted and unmodified jets.

Watching video, playing a game and broadcasting it involve different demands. A buffer can mask a short slowdown in playback. A game needs the result of an input to come back quickly from a server. A broadcast needs upload capacity. Peak download speed cannot describe all three.

A device first joins the cabin network. An aircraft terminal connects through low-Earth-orbit satellites to the wider network. Shorter radio paths can reduce propagation time. Server location, variability in delay and the number of connected devices also affect gameplay. A successful broadcast does not reveal whether every participant had consistent performance.

We see significance in the airline demonstrating a two-way use beyond reading or messaging. Its account does not quantify devices, connected users, performance on a full scheduled flight or the length of any weak connection. The event cannot establish quality at a particular seat. Check aircraft equipment and service terms for the intended journey.

The 35,000-foot figure describes the demonstration altitude. It is not an announced minimum for service. Ground and takeoff access depend on airline policies and applicable permissions. Reconnection time also matters after an interruption, but the event report does not give stage-by-stage access or recovery data.

TwitchCon runs November 13–15 in San Diego. The conference, the November 12 announcement and the fall 2027 retrofit target are separate dates. The release confirms the demonstration, while the full list of equipped scheduled flights and aircraft-by-aircraft dates remains unspecified.
