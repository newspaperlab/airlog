Vietjet launches three Vietnam flights to Cebu and Clark

The airline reports three weekly round trips on each of three nonstop routes.

Newspaper Lab · Published 2026-11-13
Source announced 2026-11-10

Vietjet’s August 17 sales notice had scheduled all three Philippine services to start November 10. The November 10 release then reported the launches, and Philstar’s November 12 report confirmed that start date. The August notice is a plan and the November reporting confirms commencement. Precise inaugural arrivals and later regular schedules require separate records.

Vietjet’s November 10 release confirms launches from Ho Chi Minh City to Cebu, Hanoi to Cebu, and Ho Chi Minh City to Clark. It reports inaugural arrivals at Mactan-Cebu and Clark international airports. The publication date does not establish the precise first departure time of every route.

Vietjet is a Vietnam-based low-cost carrier operating domestic and international services. The additions complement its Ho Chi Minh City–Manila route. Cebu gets two Vietnamese origins, while Clark gets Ho Chi Minh City. Hanoi–Clark is outside the announced set, so changing the departure city changes which nonstop is available.

Three weekly round trips apply to each route. Together they represent nine round trips, or 18 scheduled one-way sectors when both directions are counted. This excludes existing Manila service. Combining the two Cebu routes does not give a Hanoi passenger six weekly departures. Count the flights that match the actual origin and travel date.

Cebu’s airport is on Mactan, while Clark serves an area north of Manila on Luzon. Travelers heading to Manila or another airport there should allow for ground travel from Clark. People bound for Central or Northern Luzon can compare access times. Our interpretation is that the choice of arrival airport matters more directly than a network-wide flight total.

Onward connections through Vietnam need checking at booking. Separate tickets may require baggage collection and recheck, with different protections if an incoming flight runs late. Three weekly services can leave few same-day recovery options. Compare baggage, surface transport and connection time as well as the advertised fare.

Vietnamese outlet Dân trí also reported the three routes and inaugural ceremonies on November 11. The launches and announced frequencies are confirmed in the sources. Times, seasonal adjustments and fares still need a date-specific booking check.
