Emirates expands Vietnam A350 operations in two stages

The Ho Chi Minh City change is scheduled from November 1, with two Hanoi flights due to switch on December 1.

Newspaper Lab · Published 2026-11-05
Source announcement 2026-10-28

Ho Chi Minh City first

Emirates announced its plan on October 28. From November 1, it scheduled EK364/365 as a second daily A350 service between Dubai and Ho Chi Minh City. Both daily Hanoi services are due to switch on December 1. Check the city and travel date together.

Aircraft assignment versus frequency

Changing an aircraft type changes the cabin and capacity of a particular flight. It is not the same measure as adding flights to a schedule. Two daily services operated by one type indicate more chances to fly that type. The release does not establish a precise net change in daily seats.

Premium economy

The Hanoi switch introduces the intermediate cabin class in Emirates’ plan. Compare fare, baggage terms and cabin class on each sector. A through booking can mix cabins, so a headline cabin name is not enough to characterize the whole itinerary.

Dates and actual operation

November 1 and December 1 are the published deployment milestones. They do not verify each individual aircraft movement. Check the flight number and seat map for the travel date, and account for possible substitutions.

Choosing an itinerary

Our reading is that more consistent onboard products could let some passengers prioritize timing or airport access. The useful comparison still includes the Dubai connection and ground travel. Four daily services across two cities do not give every traveler four equivalent options.

Compare the whole itinerary

The A350 label does not align arrival times or guarantee the same cabin on later sectors. Compare access to the final destination, connection time and the cabin on each direction before assigning a value to the aircraft change.
