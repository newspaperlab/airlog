Qatar Airways and WestJet expand connections within Canada

The partnership adds onward options beyond Toronto and Montréal. A Qatar flight number does not necessarily identify the operating airline.

Newspaper Lab · Published 2026-11-09
Source announced 2026-10-26

Qatar Airways announced a codeshare with WestJet on October 26, adding its QR code to Canadian domestic connections. The announcement identifies onward travel through Toronto and Montréal to Vancouver, Calgary, Edmonton, Halifax, Ottawa, Regina, Saskatoon, St Johns and Winnipeg. The change links travel beyond Canada's major gateways into the partner network.

Qatar Airways operates an international connecting network centered on Doha. WestJet, a Canadian airline headquartered in Calgary, Alberta, has operated since 1996. Their networks meet at the junction between Canadian domestic travel and international journeys through Doha. This is not an announcement of new nonstop Doha services from every listed Canadian city; a journey can involve connecting flights.

The dates of the frequency increase and the partnership announcement differ. Qatar says its own Canadian services increased from 12 to 14 weekly flights from October 25, with Toronto and Montréal each served daily. That number describes Qatar's own flights, not the total of WestJet's domestic network. The two additional weekly flights do not establish daily connections to every domestic destination. The codeshare announcement followed a day later. The release does not supply one separate sales-start date covering every individual domestic sector.

Two airline identities on one flight

A codeshare lets one airline market seats under its flight number on a service operated by another airline. A segment booked with a QR number can therefore be operated by WestJet. Travelers should not infer Qatar's seats or onboard service from the marketing code alone. The operating-carrier statement and cabin information for each segment matter.

Our interpretation is that the partnership can make it easier to search for long-haul and domestic segments together. It does not guarantee a lower fare or shorter connection on every date. Actual combinations depend on timetables and fare availability. Comparing the entire journey, including airports and transfer time, is more useful than comparing the airline name alone.

Baggage and benefits need separate checks. The announcement does not establish identical baggage allowances, mileage earning or lounge access for all tickets. Nor should travelers assume separately purchased tickets receive the same connection arrangements as a through itinerary. Before payment, check each operating airline, the baggage terms shown on the ticket and the instructions for onward travel.

The official release confirms a wider connecting network, not every seasonal service or a future expansion of the agreement. Travelers with fixed dates should check what is actually bookable. The addition of domestic destinations and the increase in Qatar's own international frequencies are two separate developments. Even when both help a journey, the conditions remain specific to the booking.
