TUI lists different final dates for its Antwerp airport services

The official notice lists Tenerife on November 8 and Málaga and Alicante in March 2027. It does not announce closure of the airport.

Newspaper Lab · Published 2026-11-09
Official notice checked 2026-11-09

TUI's Belgian travel website states that its flights to and from Antwerp Airport will end from spring 2027. The final dates differ by destination: Palma de Mallorca on October 11, 2026; Tenerife on November 8; Málaga on March 24, 2027; and Alicante on March 25. We checked the notice on November 9, Korean time. The page does not show its original publication date, so this is not described as a decision announced today.

TUI is a travel group selling holidays combining flights and accommodation, with airline operations of its own. Its Belgian website has promoted regional-airport departures, offering travelers a nearby starting point for leisure trips. Future bookings need a fresh check of the departure airport. The same notice says operations from Brussels-Zaventem and Ostend will continue.

The routes do not all end on one day

The individual dates matter. A broad reference to withdrawal in spring does not mean Tenerife remains available throughout winter, nor does the November date mean every Antwerp service ends then. We have not independently checked whether the Tenerife flight listed for November 8 actually operated. A published schedule and the eventual operational record are separate pieces of information.

This is TUI's withdrawal from an airport, not an announcement that Antwerp Airport itself is closing. It does not establish that other airlines have made the same decision. Airport access and an airline's route network are different things: an airport can remain open while a desired nonstop route disappears, and a holiday sold under the same brand can use another departure airport.

Check both ends of the booking

Travelers should check outbound and return airports and dates, rather than relying on a destination name. A Brussels or Ostend alternative can change ground transport, journey time and the need for accommodation before an early departure. These are general cost implications in our analysis, not confirmation that TUI will provide free transfers or hotels to any particular passenger.

Changes and refunds for an existing booking depend on its contract and the information sent by the company. The airport information page does not establish that every booking is automatically canceled or that a particular compensation package applies. It does not set out individual replacement flights or universal compensation terms. The departure details and contact information in a traveler's own booking are the practical starting point.

The page still contains earlier promotional text about Antwerp departures alongside the withdrawal notice. The specific route-by-route end dates provide more useful guidance than that general advertising. This source does not confirm replacement services or capacity from another airline. The established change is that TUI's available departure airports will change according to the listed route schedules.
