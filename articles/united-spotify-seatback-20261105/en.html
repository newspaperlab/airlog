Inflight connectivity / EXPLAINED

United brings personal Spotify accounts to eligible seatback screens

QR pairing brings a personal library onboard. Eligible screens and connectivity are required; expansion is planned through next spring.

Newspaper Lab · Published 2026-11-06
Source announced 2026-11-05

United announced on November 5 that travelers can stream from their own Spotify accounts on eligible seatback screens. A QR code scanned with a phone pairs the account and brings in personal playlists and saved content. The change moves beyond a common airline-selected catalog toward continuing a passenger's own listening and viewing.

From a collection to an account

United is a major US airline with domestic and international hubs. Spotify offers music, podcasts and some video and audiobook content. United has carried curated Spotify programming since June 2025; the new integration adds personal libraries, recommendations, saved favorites and resumed playback. It expands an existing partnership rather than creating the first onboard Spotify offering.

Starlink powers the connection, but a Starlink aircraft does not automatically have an eligible seatback screen and the new integration. Internet equipment, entertainment hardware and app deployment are separate requirements. United plans to extend Spotify access across half its fleet by next spring. That is a rollout target, not availability across all aircraft on announcement day. Knowing that a flight has Wi-Fi alone is insufficient.

Verify on the phone

The disclosed process opens Spotify on the seatback screen, scans its QR code and verifies the account on a phone, logging in there if necessary. The phone can optionally serve as a remote. United says passengers can log out during the flight and will be logged out automatically at its end. On a shared screen, checking that the session has ended remains sensible when changing seats or finishing use, as does reading the authentication screen carefully.

Content and features depend on Spotify plan and market. Account access does not make every audiobook or video free. Subscription entitlements and regional availability can matter. The feature may reduce reliance on a phone for entertainment, but actual convenience depends on screen and connection quality. This announcement is not a hands-on test of reliability or responsiveness across routes.

United also targets fleetwide Starlink installation and more than 219,000 seatback screens by the end of 2027. That installation deadline is different from the Spotify rollout target; all entertainment features should not be assumed to arrive together. Travelers need to check eligibility for their flight and account. Keeping downloaded content provides a predictable alternative while trying the new connected service.
