A second job for the Boeing 767

Icelease agreed to one passenger-to-freighter conversion, with options for two more.

Newspaper Lab · Published 2026-11-04
Source announced 2026-11-02

One firm conversion

Boeing announced the deal on November 2. Icelease, an aircraft lessor, has agreed to convert one 767-300 and holds options on two more. The signing ceremony took place October 22.

What a conversion changes

A converted freighter uses an existing passenger airframe. A large cargo door and equipment to secure loads let the main deck carry freight. Boeing quotes 3,300 nautical miles with a 57-tonne payload. Operating conditions affect individual flights.

The Singapore timeline

The first aircraft is scheduled to enter ST Engineering’s facility in early 2027, with delivery expected in the second quarter. The options give Icelease room to respond to demand. The announcement did not name operating routes or an airline.
