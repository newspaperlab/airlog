Lufthansa Group’s profit rose despite higher fuel costs

The group reported €12.2 billion in third-quarter revenue. Here is what the headline figures capture.

Newspaper Lab · Published 2026-11-04
Source announced 2026-11-03

July to September

Lufthansa Group reported on November 3 that third-quarter revenue rose 9% to €12.2 billion. Adjusted EBIT reached around €1.4 billion, up 6%, while fuel costs rose €543 million year over year.

Reading group results

The group includes airlines beyond Lufthansa itself, plus cargo and maintenance operations. Adjusted EBIT measures operating performance after adjustments. Revenue measures sales, and net income reflects additional items.

What travellers can take from it

The company pointed to strong demand and cost control. A quarter that includes summer travel tells little about fares on a particular winter route. For a specific booking, compare schedules, change fees and baggage-inclusive prices.
