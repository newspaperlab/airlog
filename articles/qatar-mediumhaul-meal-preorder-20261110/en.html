Qatar Airways expands meal pre-order to medium-haul flights of two hours or more

First and Business passengers can choose a main course on confirmed bookings from 14 days to 24 hours before departure.

Newspaper Lab · Published 2026-11-11
Source announced 2026-11-10

Qatar Airways announced on November 10 that it has expanded meal pre-ordering to medium-haul flights of at least two hours in First and Business Class. Eligible passengers use Manage Booking on the airline’s website or app. The selection window runs from 14 days to 24 hours before departure. The release describes an expansion already made, without a separate implementation table for every route.

Qatar connects international journeys through Doha’s Hamad airport. One booking can include a long flight and a shorter connection with different services. This extends the preselection opportunity to shorter sectors, subject to the confirmed-booking and cabin requirements. A shopping-stage search provides different information from the management page after ticketing.

Passengers select their main course from the flight’s à la carte menu, which offers individual dish choices. Some destinations also have additional dishes from the Flavours to Fly For collection. The release does not establish an identical expanded menu everywhere. The booking shows the choices applicable to the traveler.

Scheduling the deadline

The 24-hour cutoff is separate from check-in. A Friday 15:00 departure points to a Thursday 15:00 selection deadline. Waiting until the previous evening may be too late. Use the time displayed in the app, especially across time zones. After changing flights close to departure, check eligibility again.

Preselection lets passengers express their preference before boarding. In our analysis, it can make preparation for connecting trips easier, but the release does not detail guarantees during ingredient or operational changes. Dietary restrictions or allergies require the relevant special-meal and safety information as well. A dish name does not list all ingredients.

If no menu appears, check the passenger’s cabin, confirmation status and remaining time first. For an agency booking, establish whether the airline booking reference gives access to the same management page.

After saving, verify the dish in Manage Booking and check each traveling companion’s selection. This is a separate service item from seat assignment. The announcement provides no comprehensive exception list or additional-fee terms. Read the actual booking alongside the cabin, flight-duration, booking-status and time-window requirements.
